# Enterprise Employee & Asset Management System 👨‍💼💻

**Enterprise Employee & Asset Management System** is a full-stack web application developed to manage employees, departments, company assets, and asset assignments in one centralized system.

The application provides role-based access and helps organizations maintain employee records, track assets, and manage asset allocation and returns.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Managing employee information and company assets manually can lead to:

* Difficulty maintaining employee records
* Lack of proper asset tracking
* Difficulty tracking assigned assets
* Manual asset allocation and return records
* Limited access control for different users

---

## 💡 Solution

The **Enterprise Employee & Asset Management System** provides a centralized platform for:

* Managing employee records
* Managing departments
* Managing company assets
* Assigning assets to employees
* Tracking asset returns
* Managing user roles and access
* Monitoring employees, assets, and assignments through a dashboard

---

## 🚀 Features

* Role-based login and access
* Employee management
* Department management
* Asset management
* Asset assignment and return
* Asset availability tracking
* Employee search and filtering
* Asset search and filtering
* Dashboard with employee and asset information
* Secure JWT authentication

## 📂 Modules Overview

* **Employee Module:** Add, update, view, search, and manage employee records
* **Department Module:** Manage organizational departments
* **Asset Module:** Add, update, view, and manage company assets
* **Assignment Module:** Assign assets to employees and track assignment history
* **Return Module:** Record returned assets and update asset availability
* **User & Role Module:** Manage users and role-based access
* **Dashboard:** View employee, asset, department, and assignment information
